**Set up your own shop and trade with other players or let villagers buy from you automatically.**



## What it does

**Trading Block** - configure up to 6 offers (item for item, your prices). Other players can browse and trade directly, no bartering needed.

**Cash Drawer** - sits next to your Trading Block and holds payment. If it's full or missing, trades stop going through — so your shop can't overflow or get griefed while you're offline.

**Villagers** - will automatically buy items you've priced in emeralds, up to a price cap so they don't accidentally empty out a stall meant for players.

**Ownership** - whoever places a Trading Block or Cash Drawer owns it. Only the owner can change offers or grab what's stored inside.

## Setup

Place a Cash Drawer, place a Trading Block on top of it, right-click to set your offers, and you're open for business.


## Requires

- Fabric API
- REI is supported if you have it installed, but not required
